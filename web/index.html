generative-station

Music that is made as it plays and does not end. None of it is a recording: a program is playing this now, and will not play it the same way again.

Its notes come from a chemical compound. A mass spectrometer breaks a compound into pieces and weighs them, and what it finds is a row of peaks: one for each weight of piece, taller the more pieces there were of that weight. Every peak is made a pitch, louder the taller it is, and the station's voices take their notes from those. The pictures are drawn from the same peaks.

It has no compound at the moment, so its voices are choosing their own notes.

The compound is the last one that somebody turned into sound with the mass spectrum to audio converter. Choose one there and within a minute the station is playing it, for everyone who is listening. The word after the compound's name on this page (linear, inverse or modulo) is the rule that was chosen there for turning weights into pitches.

The spectra are from MassBank, © the MassBank consortium, CC BY 4.0. How the station is made is on GitHub.
