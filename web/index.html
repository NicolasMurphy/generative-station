generative-station

Generative music that never stops. Nothing is recorded; a program is playing it live and won't play it the same way twice.

The notes come from a chemical compound. A mass spectrometer breaks a compound into fragments and weighs them, producing a set of peaks. Each peak becomes a pitch, louder for taller peaks. The visuals are drawn from the same peaks.

No compound right now, so the voices are picking their own notes.

The compound is whichever one was most recently sent from the mass spectrum to audio converter. Pick one there and the station switches to it within a minute, for everyone listening. The word after the compound's name (linear, inverse or modulo) is the mapping from mass to pitch.
